Privacy Policy

Gentlemen's FC (The Extraordinary Gentlemen's Flight Club), by OPYian
Effective date: 13 November 2026

This policy explains what happens to your information when you play Gentlemen's FC (“the game”) on Android or in a web browser. The short version: the game has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics, and we do not collect personal data.

Information we do not collect

  • No registration, login or account of any kind.
  • No advertising, no advertising ID and no tracking.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, files or location.

Information stored on your device

To let you continue a race and remember your preferences, the game stores a small amount of data on your own device only: the race in progress, sound settings, which race types you have already seen, and similar game settings. This data is never sent to us. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the game.

Playing on several devices (online multiplayer)

When the online multiplayer feature is available and you choose to use it, devices in the same race connect directly to each other. To find each other, they use a public connection service (the PeerJS signalling server, operated by a third party). For a connection to work:

  • your device's IP address and a random room code are visible to that connection service and to the other devices in your race;
  • the names you type for your aviators and the moves of the race are sent to the other players in that race.

We do not run that service and we do not receive or store any of this information. If you do not use online multiplayer, no connection of this kind is made. Playing against computer players or passing one device around never sends anything over the internet.

If you join a race by scanning an invite QR code, the camera is used only on your device to read the code. No image is stored or sent anywhere.

Purchases

The game is currently free and offers no purchases. If an optional purchase is added in the future, it will be handled entirely by Google Play, and we will not receive your payment details. This policy will be updated before any such change.

Children

The game is designed for teenagers and adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.
